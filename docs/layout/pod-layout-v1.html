Wire Guide

Jumpers — 22 AWG solid core, colored by net
Strip ~5 mm, tin the end, lay it flat on the pad, then solder. Route along the grid rather than diagonally where you can — easier to trace later.
Bridges — no wire
Only between neighboring pads. If solder won't span the gap, lay a clipped resistor-lead offcut across both pads.
Edge pads (Y2–Y9) — jumper and pigtail together
Each edge pad takes one jumper now and one pigtail wire at the end. Park the jumper on the pad (through its hole if it has one, otherwise tinned and laid flat), bend it to hold, and solder only when the pigtail wire goes in. Work from the back — C1's body crowds the front of the pads.
Stranded wire — everything that leaves the board
SW1 leads, MT3608 wires, connector pigtails (J1 battery, J2 LED strip, J3 elbow). Survives flexing.

57LED strip connector J2 (JST-SM 3-pin): use the mate to the strip's input plug — the arrows on the strip point away from the pod.
